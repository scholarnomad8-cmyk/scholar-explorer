FROM IDEA TO OFFER

Your application journey

Use this checklist as a roadmap; follow the current programme rules first.

01

Explore & shortlist

  • Choose country, degree level and subject.
  • Check nationality, age, GPA and graduation rules.
  • Compare benefits, language rules and deadlines.
  • Save official links in a deadline calendar.
02

Prepare your profile

  • Organize transcripts and certificates.
  • Prepare CV and achievement list.
  • Build projects, volunteering or research experience.
  • Plan language tests early if required.
03

Documents

  • Passport and identity documents, if required.
  • Transcripts, certificates and grading scale.
  • Motivation letter, study plan or research proposal.
  • Recommendation letters, CV, portfolio.
  • Certified translation / attestation if specified.
04

Submit carefully

  • Answer every prompt honestly and directly.
  • Tailor essays to the specific programme.
  • Follow file formats, size and naming rules.
  • Request references well before the deadline.
  • Save confirmation and submitted copies.
05

Interview & selection

  • Research the scholarship and host institution.
  • Practise goals, subject choice and impact plans.
  • Prepare examples of leadership and problem-solving.
  • Check email and portal; beware of fake offers.
06

After the offer

  • Read all conditions and acceptance deadlines.
  • Confirm funding and payment schedule in writing.
  • Follow official visa and medical instructions.
  • Ask about insurance, housing and arrival.
  • Keep copies of the signed offer.

FROM HOME TO CAMPUS

Study-abroad survival guide

Small details students often miss. Immigration, customs and university rules vary by country.

🛂 Visa & documents

Use the embassy/immigration checklist. Check passport validity, visa category, financial proof, insurance, medical requirements and processing time. Keep paper and digital copies separately.

✈️ Flights & baggage

Confirm visa, arrival window, university start date, baggage limits and whether airfare is paid upfront or reimbursed later. Share your itinerary with your university and family.

🧳 Packing

Keep originals in hand luggage. Pack essentials, prescriptions in original packaging where appropriate, adapters and emergency contacts. Check customs rules before packing medicine or food.

🛬 At the airport

Follow immigration signs, answer questions truthfully, keep your admission letter and accommodation address accessible, collect baggage and use official transport or pre-arranged university pickup.

🏠 Dormitory & housing

Confirm move-in dates, address, deposit, utilities, bedding, kitchen rules and check-in hours. Ask what happens if your flight arrives after office hours. Verify any deposit recipient before paying.

📱 First 72 hours

Check in to housing, contact your university, arrange a SIM/eSIM if useful, learn emergency numbers and transit, complete required registration and locate campus support, groceries and a clinic.

🏦 Money & budget

Budget for food, transport, phone, books and personal costs. Learn local payment methods, bank requirements, scholarship payment timing and emergency support options.

🩺 Health & safety

Understand health insurance, prescriptions, emergency numbers and campus support. Keep emergency contacts offline and know how to contact your embassy or consulate if needed.

🎓 Campus life

Attend orientation, meet your academic adviser, register for courses, learn attendance and academic-integrity rules and find libraries, labs, tutoring and international-student support.

This is general educational guidance, not immigration or legal advice. Follow official government, airline and university instructions for your destination.
